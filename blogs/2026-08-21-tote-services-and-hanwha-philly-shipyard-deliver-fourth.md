TOTE Services and Hanwha Philly Shipyard Deliver Fourth National Security Multi-Mission Vessel, the LONE STAR STATE PHILADELPHIA (August 21, 2026) — TOTE Services, LLC, a leading U.S. maritime services company specializing in vessel construction management and ship management, together with Hanwha Philly Shipyard, Inc. (HPSI), has completed the fourth of five National Security Multi-Mission Vessels (NSMV), the LONE STAR STATE.
