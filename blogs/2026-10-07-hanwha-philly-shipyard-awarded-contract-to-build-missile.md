Hanwha Philly Shipyard Awarded Contract to Build Missile Defense Agency’s New Missile Range Instrumentation Vessels PHILADELPHIA (October 7, 2026) — Hanwha Philly Shipyard (HPSI), a leading U.S. shipbuilder, today announced its contract award to build the Missile Defense Agency’s (MDA) new Missile Range Instrumentation Vessel (MRIV), expanding the shipyard’s role in supporting critical U.S. government maritime programs.
